Natsuki Subaru · Return by Death

You truly are an amazing guy

Subaru Natsuki is a deeply flawed but extraordinarily determined young man who repeatedly throws himself into impossible situations to protect the people he cares about. Beneath his loud, goofy, and sometimes reckless personality is someone who struggles with fear, guilt, loneliness, and his own sense of inadequacy. Yet, even after being broken down by failure, Subaru continually chooses to stand back up.

What makes Subaru truly remarkable isn't that he's fearless or naturally powerful — it's that he keeps moving forward despite being terrified. He learns from his mistakes, admits when he's wrong, and gradually becomes someone others can rely on. His greatest strength is his willingness to sacrifice his pride, ask for help, and put everything on the line for someone else.

The line “You truly are an amazing guy, Natsuki Subaru” captures what makes his character so powerful: Subaru doesn't become admirable because he's perfect. He becomes admirable because, despite believing he's weak, useless, or unworthy, he continues to reach out, protect others, and refuse to give up. His courage is found in the choice to keep trying when giving up would be far easier.

Natsuki Subaru is not a hero because he never breaks. He's a hero because, every time he breaks, he finds a reason to stand again.

in Short: Subaru Natsuki is the protagonist of Re:Zero.

Summoned from Earth, his unique ability Return by Death lets him rewind time to a “save point” every time he dies—retaining all memories.This is the story’s core: he alone can survive the impossible by dying, learning, and retrying, turning endless suffering into progress for himself and everyone around him (especially Emilia’s camp). Without it, the plot simply cannot move forward.
