Natsuki Subaru · Return by Death · Emilia's Knight

From Flawed Shut-in to Unyielding Vanguard of Season 1

In Season 1, Natsuki Subaru starts as an overly confident, delusional shut-in who believes being summoned to another world makes him an automatic fantasy protagonist. Unprepared for the brutal reality of Lugunica, his entitlement and arrogance quickly shatter as he faces horrific deaths, agonizing trauma, and a public, humiliating breakdown at the Royal Selection capital that severs his bond with Emilia.

The core of Subaru's Season 1 standing lies in his painful descent to rock bottom and his eventual rebirth. Unable to explain Return by Death to anyone, he suffers in complete isolation until Rem's unwavering belief in him during Episode 18 ("From Zero") forces him to abandon his self-loathing, drop his foolish pride, and start over from scratch as a true leader.

By the end of the season, Subaru transforms into a tactical mastermind through sheer willpower. He coordinates the historic subjugation of the White Whale alongside Wilhelm, rallies rival factions under Crusch and Anastasia, and defeats the terrifying Sin Archbishop of Sloth, Petelgeuse Romanee-Conti, earning his place as Emilia's knight.

At his core in Season 1, Subaru proves that true strength isn't about physical power or magic—it is the courage to face your own flaws, ask others for help, and stand back up after being broken to save the people you love.

In Short: Subaru Natsuki is the protagonist of Re:Zero Season 1.

Initially a foolish shut-in, he undergoes a painful emotional evolution through Return by Death, transitioning from an arrogant child into a self-sacrificing strategist who unites royal candidates to defeat the White Whale and Sloth.

  • Thinks kissing automatically gets someone pregnant
